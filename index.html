A window into
a web of the past.

This page brings together the names, categories, and prices visible in a preserved Myanmar Mart storefront. The archive records a small selection of paintings and mascot pieces associated with the 2013 SEA Games.

It is an independent historical recreation. Myanmar Mart’s original site and this page are not represented as a current business or an active store.
